           Abstract     The authors present a new theoretical        approach   for a general model of non-linear      chromate-
           graphy in the presence of axial dispersion and mass transfer resistances, based on perturbation               analysis.
           They first derive aproximate     analytical expressions for steady (constant-pattern)       fronts, which allow them
           to clarify the empirical idea that the HTUs (height of transfer units) for mass transfer and axial dispersion are
           additive. This result is in fact an approximation      for which they derive an applicability criterion. They also
           show how the concentration      of the stoichiometric    point of the constant pattern front depends on the kinetic
           mechanism.     Computation    of higher-order    terms allows them to derive an approximation         to the transient
           front, which, by comparison     with numerical and experimental         results, has been found to be very accurate.
